# 2026-08-29: Contact Infrastructure Decision

## Formspree Contact Backend Decision

### Context

The `Work With Me` form requires a backend endpoint because `grantgeist.com` is currently hosted as a static site on GitHub Pages.

Formspree already provides that function and is implemented, tested, and working. The new `hello@grantgeist.com` business email gives the site a direct public email address, but it does not replace the form backend.

### Decision

Keep Formspree for the homepage contact form.

Use `hello@grantgeist.com` directly where normal email interaction is preferable, including the virtual business card.

Do not replace Formspree with Google Apps Script or move hosting solely to remove this dependency.

### Constraint

Formspree's free tier currently allows **50 submissions per month across an unlimited number of forms and projects**.

This is sufficient for current expected traffic.

### Revisit When

Reconsider the contact backend if:

- submissions begin approaching 50/month;
- Formspree pricing or limits materially change;
- spam or reliability becomes a problem;
- the site develops broader backend requirements;
- hosting moves away from GitHub Pages.

At that point, compare:

1. paying for Formspree;
2. a small Google Apps Script endpoint;
3. handling contact submission inside a future application backend, potentially alongside a hosting change such as Replit.

Avoid introducing an intermediate backend architecture without a concrete need.
